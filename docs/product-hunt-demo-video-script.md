# WishCard Product Hunt Demo Video Script

> Date: 2026-05-14
> Target length: 45-60 seconds
> Primary URL: `/demo/product-hunt`
> Backup visual source: `/demo/product-hunt/gallery`

## 1. Video Goal

The video must make one idea obvious:

WishCard is not a chore tracker. It is an AI family planner that turns family routines into daily actions, stars, real wishes, parent promises, and weekly reviews.

## 2. Format

- Length: 45-60 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Resolution: 1920x1080 preferred
- Captions: burned in or uploaded as subtitles
- Voice: warm, clear, parent-friendly
- Pace: fast enough for Product Hunt, calm enough for a parenting product
- First product UI appearance: within 5 seconds

## 3. Core Voiceover

Use this as the primary 55-second narration:

```text
Family life does not usually break from one big problem.
It breaks from planning, reminding, checking, and renegotiating the same routines every day.

WishCard is an AI family planner that turns a family profile into a realistic weekly rhythm.

For Emma and Leo, it builds an after-school plan around homework, reading, piano, and bedtime.

Then WishCard turns that plan into kid-sized daily tasks with clear rewards.

Leo completes tasks, earns stars, and uses those stars for something meaningful: a Saturday museum trip.

When a wish is redeemed, WishCard creates a parent promise, so the reward becomes real.

At the end of the week, parents get a review of wins, pressure points, and what to adjust next.

WishCard helps families plan routines, motivate kids, and fulfill wishes.
```

## 6. Recording Checklist

Before recording:

- Run local app or production preview.
- Open `/demo/product-hunt`.
- Reset the demo state.
- Use a desktop viewport close to 1440x1000 or browser full screen.
- Hide bookmarks bar and unrelated browser UI when possible.
- Confirm browser zoom is 100%.
- Confirm no dev overlay or console error is visible.
- Confirm `Start the demo`, `Complete task`, and `Redeem wish` work.

During recording:

- Use smooth cursor movement.
- Pause 1-2 seconds after each important state change.
- Avoid scrolling too quickly.
- Keep text readable.
- Do not show terminal or local file paths.
- Do not show Chinese UI in the video.

After recording:

- Watch once muted and confirm captions tell the story.
- Watch once with audio and confirm no section feels rushed.
- Confirm first product UI appears within 5 seconds.
- Confirm the wish loop appears before 40 seconds.
- Confirm final frame includes product name and tagline.

## 7. Production Notes

Recommended music:

- Light, warm, low-volume.
- Avoid childish jingles.
- Avoid dramatic startup-style music.

Recommended voice style:

- Calm product narrator.
- Parent-aware, not hype-driven.
- Confident but not salesy.

Recommended editing:

- Add subtle zooms into key cards.
- Use simple cuts between steps.
- Avoid complex motion graphics.
- Keep captions high contrast.
